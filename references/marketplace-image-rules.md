# Marketplace image rules

Checked: **2026-09-29**. Recheck before a real experiment: public documentation does not establish account eligibility or guarantee moderation. Do not apply another seller region's subscription limits silently.

## Wildberries: verified public requirements

[Official photo rules, updated 2026-08-28](https://seller.wildberries.ru/instructions/ru/am/material/item-photo-rules-recommendations-and-common-mistakes): JPG, PNG or WEBP; at least 700 × 900 px; compression quality at least 65%; at most 10 MB. The article does not establish a universal required aspect ratio or recommended resolution above that minimum.

Keep the complete product visible, sharp and undistorted; use a clean neutral contrasting background. Show the complete set on the first photo when selling a set. Avoid extraneous promotional graphics, tags and logos. Watermarks, distracting text, vivid badges, collages and poor AI-rendered details are listed as quality/ranking problems. Minimal neutral infographics are recommended, not universally prohibited. Lifestyle imagery is conditional on the product remaining clear. Do not translate all ranking recommendations into automatic upload bans. Preserve genuine branding physically present on the product; do not invent or overlay other brands. Exact universal prohibitions on prices, contacts and each phrase (“хит”, “акция”, “бесплатно”) were not individually established by this page; the skill conservatively excludes them.

## Wildberries: native experiment

[Official native A/B guide](https://seller.wildberries.ru/instructions/ru/ru/material/main-photo-ab-testing) and [readable localized version, updated 2026-07-20](https://seller.wildberries.ru/instructions/ka/am/material/main-photo-ab-testing) describe the current main photo against **one** uploaded challenger, with a limited visitor cohort split approximately 50/50. Public documentation requires a Jam subscription; localized terms list monthly included test counts by tariff. Verify the actual account's entitlement rather than quoting those counts as universal.

The creation form accepts a future start date and a duration from one week to two months. Do not change the main photo during the test; maintain stock. Route through the account's Products and prices area and its A/B section, select the SKU, current A and challenger B, then schedule after seller approval. Advertising can be attached separately, but this skill must not initiate spend or change advertising mid-test. A second challenger requires a separate preregistered test, not a fictitious three-arm native experiment.

Daily per-variant orders, carts, randomized-unit counts and downloadable export schema were **not verified** in this research. Inspect the actual export; never substitute overall SKU orders into both variants. Without attributed outcomes, do not declare an orders/impressions winner.

## Ozon: verification gap, not an assertion of absence

These official entry points were attempted on the checked date:

- [Global media requirements](https://docs.ozon.ru/global/products/requirements/media/) — browser returned a redirect loop.
- [Image requirements](https://docs.ozon.ru/global/products/requirements/media/image-requirements/) — browser returned an internal retrieval error.
- [Seller education image requirements](https://seller-edu.ozon.ru/work-with-goods/trebovaniya-k-kartochkam-tovarov/izobrazheniya-tovara) — content unavailable through the browser.

Consequently **aspect ratio, minimum/recommended resolution, accepted formats, file limits, category/background exceptions, infographic restrictions and native photo A/B access/metrics remain unverified here**. Conflicting third-party claims were not promoted to requirements. Do not label Ozon images “platform compliant” or claim that Ozon has no native testing tool.

Before Ozon publication, consult the seller account's current media rules for the exact category and record the source/date. Native randomized testing can only be planned after evidence of account availability, assignment method and attributed metrics is supplied. Otherwise use a explicitly lower-confidence sequential plan; never present it as randomized.

Seller-facing disclosure: «Актуальные правила Ozon и доступность встроенного A/B не удалось подтвердить по первоисточнику. Это рабочий макет, не подтверждение прохождения модерации. Перед публикацией сверь правила категории в кабинете».

## Skill output profile and content guardrails

**Implementation choice, not an official marketplace recommendation:** 900 × 1200 px (3:4), JPEG in sRGB, quality 95. Check the final file is under 10 MB. This exceeds the verified WB minimum. For Ozon it is only a draft profile until category rules are confirmed. If the category requires another aspect ratio, create a matching profile before paid generation; do not crop the product to force a fit.

No prices, discounts, contact details, URLs/QR codes, seller watermarks, added third-party logos, unsupported superlatives or “хит/акция/бесплатно” promotional badges. These are conservative skill constraints where the precise platform prohibition is unverified. One factual USP and at most one factual badge remain hypotheses, subject to category rules; a badge must not imply marketplace endorsement. Competitor screenshots are private comparison inputs, never generation references for copying another product or brand.

Source refresh protocol: record the exact official URL, check date, seller region/category and whether the claim is a requirement, recommendation or unknown. Login-only information needs seller-provided evidence or authorized read-only inspection, not invented certainty.
